Harborlight Logistics Employee Handbook (fictional sample document for evaluation)

1. Company overview

Harborlight Logistics was founded in 2011 in Tallinn, Estonia, by Mari Kask and Jonas Berg. The company operates cold-chain warehouses for pharmaceutical distributors. Its headquarters moved to Riga, Latvia, in 2019. Harborlight runs four warehouses: Riga North, Riga Port, Vilnius East, and Tartu Central.

2. Working hours and remote work

Standard office hours are 08:30 to 17:00, Monday to Friday. Office staff may work remotely up to two days per week with approval from their team lead. Warehouse staff work in three rotating shifts and are not eligible for remote work.

3. Leave policy

Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused leave days may be carried over into the next year; any remaining unused days expire on 31 March. Leave requests must be submitted through the StaffDesk portal at least 10 working days in advance. Sick leave requires a doctor's certificate after the third consecutive day of absence.

4. Temperature control procedures

All pharmaceutical storage zones must be kept between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius. Sensors log the temperature every 5 minutes. If a zone stays outside the allowed range for more than 15 minutes, the shift supervisor must move the affected stock to the backup cold room and file an incident report within 24 hours. The backup cold room in each warehouse is tested every Monday at 07:00.

5. Expenses and travel

Business travel must be booked through the company travel desk. The daily meal allowance for travel is 45 euros within the Baltic states and 60 euros elsewhere. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the trip with itemised receipts attached. Claims above 500 euros require approval from the finance director.

6. Safety training

Every new warehouse employee completes a two-day safety induction before their first shift. Forklift operators must renew their certification every 3 years. Fire drills are held twice a year, in April and October.
